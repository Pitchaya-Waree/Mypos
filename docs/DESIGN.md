## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for modern cloud point-of-sale platforms, inventory management engines, and high-velocity commerce SaaS. It reflects the agile operational speed of BoothMate, the robust transactional reliability of Loyverse POS, and the polished product-led growth aesthetics of MakerGO.

### Personality & Tone
- **Precision & High Utility:** Uncluttered layouts designed for intense transactional environments, split-second cashier inputs, and deep administrative analytics.
- **Tech-Forward & Dynamic:** Electric blue accents and hyper-clean surfaces communicate real-time synchronization, cutting-edge hardware integration, and intelligent automation.
- **Approachable Enterprise:** Balances rigorous compliance and enterprise durability with the intuitive, tactile warmth of modern consumer-grade software.

### Visual Style Direction
A refined blend of **Modern SaaS Functionalism** and **Tactile Micro-Surfacing**:
- **Structured Architectural Cards:** Distinct panel dividers, crisp light outlines, and subtle cool-slate backing layers build immediate visual hierarchy.
- **Micro-Gradients & Accents:** Controlled applications of electric cyan-blue gradients mark system vitality, active status indicators, and prime transaction actions.
- **Information Density Control:** Dynamic scaling allows high-density transaction pads (keypads, cart manifests, SKU tables) to coexist with breathable executive metrics and setup flows.

## Colors

The palette leverages a high-contrast spectrum rooted in pure whites, slate neutrals, deep royal blues, and reactive sky-blue accents.

### Color Architecture
- **Primary (`#1E40AF`):** Deep, electric royal blue for primary system triggers, header branding, critical CTAs, active checkout buttons, and authoritative metric highlights.
- **Secondary (`#0EA5E9`):** Bright, luminous sky blue for interactive focus rings, active states, progress indicators, SaaS status badges, and subtle data telemetry graphics.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`):** Precision emerald green dedicated to successful transactions, live hardware connectivity, approved settlements, and net revenue growth.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`):** Rich slate-black for typography and high-priority data points, delivering AA/AAA contrast ratios against light backgrounds.

### Surface Tones & Backgrounds
- **Canvas Base:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for card modules, data grid cells, and terminal viewports.
- **App Canvas / Frame:** `#F8FAFC` to establish structural depth behind interactive surfaces.
- **Muted Structural Layer:** `#F1F5F9` for table headers, disabled states, terminal dividers, and secondary badge containers.
- **Border / Divider System:** `#E2E8F0` for crisp boundaries; `#CBD5E1` for active or focused boundaries.

## Typography

The typography uses Inter across display, body, and transactional elements, capitalizing on its tall x-height, neutral balance, and open apertures for maximum legibility on digital POS monitors, tablets, and handheld scanners.

### Hierarchy & Style Rules
- **Numerical Data & Metrics:** Use `data-metric` with tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums;`) to prevent jitter across live transaction tallies, receipt sums, and analytics counters.
- **Form & Input Labels:** Use `label-md` or `label-sm` in semi-bold or bold, positioned strictly above inputs to reduce cognitive friction during fast point-of-sale entries.
- **SaaS Badges & Metadata:** Set labels in `label-sm` with slight positive tracking (`0.04em`) to ensure instant readability in compact status pills.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an adaptable **12-column fluid grid** for management consoles and a **dual/triple panel split** for live POS terminal modes.

### Breakpoints & Layout Adapters
- **Desktop / Admin Hub (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid with `gutter-lg` (2rem) and `margin-lg` (3rem). Dedicated persistent sidebar (260px) and contextual utility rail.
- **Tablet / Terminal Mode (768px – 1279px):** Split-view architecture with `gutter` (1.5rem) and `margin` (2rem). Left canvas: catalog/keypad matrix (60% width); Right canvas: fixed cart/receipt ledger (40% width).
- **Mobile / Companion App (< 768px):** Single-column stacked layout with `gutter-sm` (1rem) and `margin-sm` (1rem). Checkout summary collapses to a sticky bottom sheet with immediate tap targets.

### Spacing Principles
- Minimum touch target for touch-enabled point-of-sale views is `48px` × `48px`, utilizing internal padding of `space-md`.
- Grouping logic uses `space-xs` and `space-sm` for strictly bound elements (e.g., input with field prefix), and `space-lg` to separate modular functional cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid of **low-contrast micro-outlines** and **diffused slate shadows**, maintaining high contrast without visual clutter.

### Elevation Levels
- **Base Tier (Flat / Surface):** Background `#F8FAFC`, cards `#FFFFFF` with a 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border. No drop shadow. Used for standard dashboard modules and catalog item tiles.
- **Resting Interactive (`elevation-1`):** `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)` combined with a 1px border in `#E2E8F0`. Used for data cards, primary input fields, and metric widgets.
- **Hover / Active Tier (`elevation-2`):** `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`, border color transitions to `#CBD5E1`. Used for hoverable catalog items and card action buttons.
- **Elevated Surfaces / Floating (`elevation-3`):** `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Used for checkout modals, sticky checkout sum rails, and active autocomplete dropdowns.
- **Terminal Overlays (`elevation-4`):** `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` over a 40% slate backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-sm`). Used for tender processing and payment success takeovers.

## Shapes

The shape system employs level `2` (Rounded) geometry, balancing crisp architectural discipline with modern ergonomics:
- **Default Elements (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Buttons, standard inputs, table containers, metric cards, and SKU tiles.
- **Large Panels (`rounded-lg` / `1rem` / `16px`):** Checkout receipt cards, terminal register wrappers, modal dialogues, and analytics chart panels.
- **Pills / Status Containers (`rounded-full`):** SaaS product indicators, system status dots, and filter chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1E40AF` with white typography, 8px border radius, medium shadow, 0.5s border-box transition. On hover: `#1D4ED8`. Focus: 3px ring in `#0EA5E9` with 2px offset.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** White background, 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, `#0F172A` text. On hover: `#F8FAFC` background with `#CBD5E1` border.
- **Terminal Action / Checkout:** Background `#0EA5E9` with crisp white text, bold font-weight, elevated padding (`space-md` vertical, `space-lg` horizontal) to serve as the dominant register action.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Standard height: 44px (48px for POS touchscreens).
- Background: `#FFFFFF` with 1px border `#E2E8F0`. 
- Focus state: border turns `#0EA5E9` accompanied by a `0 0 0 3px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.15)` halo.
- Prefix/Suffix integration: slate-tinted icons (`#64748B`) inside the input container for barcode scanning triggers or currency denominations.

### SaaS Badges & Chips
- Status badges use muted 10% tinted backgrounds with saturated text:
  - **Live/Online:** `#ECFDF5` background with `#047857` text and a pulsing emerald dot.
  - **Syncing/Pending:** `#F0F9FF` background with `#0369A1` text.
  - **Offline/Critical:** `#FEF2F2` background with `#B91C1C` text.
- Filter chips: 32px height, fully rounded or 8px radius, white surface, `#E2E8F0` border; turns `#1E40AF` text and `#EFF6FF` background when active.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 20px × 20px bounding box with 6px radius (checkbox) or circle (radio).
- Unchecked: `#FFFFFF` with 1.5px border `#CBD5E1`.
- Checked: `#1E40AF` fill with sharp white check/dot icon. Focus displays secondary cyan ring.

### Cards & Metrics
- Metric Cards: Pure white surface, 16px radius, subtle border (`#E2E8F0`), padding `space-lg`. Display metric in `data-metric` (`#0F172A`), subtitle in `body-sm` (`#64748B`), and trend delta pill in top-right corner.
- Cart & Manifest Cards: Divided rows with 1px border-bottom in `#F1F5F9`, zebra-free for scanability, with rapid +/- touch steppers on the right rail.

### Terminal & Register Mockup Components
- **Cart Ledger:** Fixed-width container with total calculation anchored to the bottom. Uses prominent price typography with high-contrast breakdown of tax, discounts, and net total.
- **Hardware Status Bar:** Pinned slim top bar displaying battery life, printer status, scanner sync, and cloud connection with micro-pills.
